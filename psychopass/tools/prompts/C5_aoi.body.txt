1. id "cg_house": Night in the tatami room of an old Japanese countryside house, almost completely dark. On the right, the hologram of Akane’s grandmother (reference 2) sits in seiza in front of a wooden Buddhist family altar, glowing a soft warm amber, the only light in the room. On the far left, Akane (reference 1, in her suit and a dark coat) stands frozen in the dark hallway, half in shadow, looking at her. Quiet horror.
2. id "cg_bad_room": A dark tatami back room at dawn, a strip of cold light through a gap in the shoji. Akane (reference 1) sits at a low table, seen from behind, completely still, head slightly bowed; across the table the hologram grandmother (reference 2) smiles warmly at her; a teacup sits in front of Akane. Silent, tragic, nothing gory.
3. id "cg_title": Key visual for the title screen. Night, a cold blue near-future Tokyo skyline with soft holographic lights. On the LEFT third of the image, Tsunemori Akane (reference 1) stands in her suit, holding a Dominator lowered at her side, looking back over her shoulder toward the viewer with a calm, unreadable face. Behind her, huge and faint, a translucent hologram of her grandmother (reference 2) dissolves upward into light particles. Leave the RIGHT half of the image relatively empty and dark for the title logo.
